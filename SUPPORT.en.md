## Before you post

- Check the FAQ and the error code table in the [README](README.en.md) first.
- Never post student IDs, passwords or other personal data. Use placeholders.
- For bugs, include the language and version, the method you called and the error code.
- This is a personal open source project, not an official Sejong University service. Answers come from volunteers and may take time.

Participants follow the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.en.md).
