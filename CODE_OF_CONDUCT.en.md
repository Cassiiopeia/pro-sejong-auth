# Code of Conduct

[한국어](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) | English

This is a short draft based on the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want an open community where everyone can take part free from harassment, regardless of age, disability, gender, student status, nationality, appearance, religion or any similar trait.

## Encouraged behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Give constructive feedback and receive it with an open mind.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologize and learn from them.
- Focus on the code and ideas, not on people.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Insults, put-downs, discrimination, harassment or threats
- Sexual language or imagery and unwelcome advances
- Publishing someone else's personal information (including student IDs and passwords) without consent
- Anything else that is inappropriate in a public community

## Scope

This applies to every space of this project: issues, pull requests, comments and Discussions.

## Reporting and enforcement

Please report unacceptable behavior to the repository owner. Instead of a public issue, send a [private report](https://github.com/Cassiiopeia/pro-sejongpeia/security/advisories/new) (Security tab > Report a vulnerability) with `[Code of Conduct]` at the start of the title. Only the repository owner can see it.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that go against this code, and may limit participation after repeated problems. Reporters' details are kept private.
